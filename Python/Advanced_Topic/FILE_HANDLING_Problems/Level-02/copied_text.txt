A SHARP RAZOR BLADE AND A HEAVY IRON AXE LIVED IN A CARPENTER'S WORKSHOP.
ONE DAY, THE RAZOR BLADE MOCKED THE AXE. 
IT SAID, "LOOK AT HOW THIN, SLEEK, AND SHARP I AM. EVERYONE PRAISES MY FINE EDGE. 
YOU ARE HEAVY, CRUDE, AND DULL BY COMPARISON.
THE AXE SMILED AND SAID NOTHING.
JUST THEN, THE CARPENTER PICKED UP THE RAZOR BLADE TO TRIM A THICK PIECE OF WOOD.
 THE TINY BLADE PRESSED HARD AGAINST THE TIMBER, BUT ITS EDGE IMMEDIATELY BENT AND DULLED AGAINST THE ROUGH GRAIN, FAILING TO MAKE A DENT.
 FRUSTRATED, THE CARPENTER SET IT ASIDE AND PICKED UP THE HEAVY AXE. 
 WITH A FEW POWERFUL SWINGS, THE AXE CHOPPED THE THICK LOG RIGHT DOWN THE MIDDLE, SHAPING IT INTO A USEFUL BEAM.
 THE BRUISED RAZOR BLADE LOOKED UP FROM THE FLOOR, FINALLY UNDERSTANDING.
